# Existing-literature demo stabilization design

Date: 2026-08-22

## Purpose and scope

Prepare a repeatable, reliable demonstration of the integrated existing-literature workflow:

1. collect existing literature online;
2. download open PDFs;
3. parse full text;
4. extract knowledge with the configured LLM;
5. display complete records in OpenIsle;
6. submit six-dimensional evaluations through registered OpenIsle users; and
7. produce formal paper and author rankings.

OpenIsle submission drafts and submitted manuscripts remain outside this demonstration corpus. No local-PDF
import is required.

## Acceptance criteria

- At least 30 existing-literature records have an accessible open PDF and pass the completeness audit.
- Processing status is explicit for metadata, PDF download, parsing and knowledge extraction.
- Recoverable failures can be retried and permanent failures expose a concrete reason.
- OpenIsle does not present incomplete records as if they were complete.
- At least 10 literature detail pages receive a visual and data-quality check.
- At least 5 complete papers receive 10 or more evaluations and become `FORMAL`.
- Paper and author ranking pages contain real results generated by the production scoring service.
- Docker restart preserves literature, processing and rating data.
- A final report records corpus counts, success rate, failure reasons, demo accounts and demonstration route.

## Architecture

The current service boundaries remain unchanged.

### Collection layer

- Use multiple predefined topics and open sources, prioritizing arXiv, PMC, Crossref and OpenAlex.
- Continue collecting until at least 30 papers with downloadable open PDFs pass the audit.
- Deduplicate by DOI, source identifier and normalized title.
- A metadata-only record does not count toward the successful demonstration corpus.

### Processing layer

- Run PDF download, full-text parsing, knowledge-card extraction, methods extraction and data-point extraction as
  separately observable stages.
- Persist stage state and the latest failure reason.
- Retry transient network, rate-limit, PDF download, LLM timeout and truncated-JSON failures a bounded number of
  times.
- Mark paywalls, missing open PDFs, non-PDF payloads and invalid identifiers as terminal failures.
- Resume interrupted work after container restart.

### Presentation layer

- Show processing state and content completeness in the OpenIsle literature list.
- Prefer fully processed papers in the normal demonstration view.
- Render an explicit explanation for missing PDF, full text or extracted artifacts instead of an empty panel.
- Summarize total, complete, failed and retryable records in the administration workflow.
- Preserve the literature service's `paper_id` through list, detail, PDF, extracted content and rating links.

### Evaluation demonstration layer

- Provide an idempotent development/demo seeding tool, not a public UI button or production endpoint.
- Create approximately 12 registered demo users distributed across at least 4 institution domains.
- Select at least 5 complete papers and submit 10–12 varied six-dimensional ratings with written assessments for
  each paper.
- Write every rating through the normal OpenIsle rating service so validation, snapshots, formal eligibility and
  rankings use production business logic.
- Re-running the tool updates the same demo users and ratings instead of duplicating them.

## Data-quality rules

A paper counts as complete when:

- title and author information are present;
- an open PDF is available through OpenIsle;
- parsed full text exceeds a defensible minimum length;
- at least one substantive section or body representation exists;
- a knowledge card exists;
- methods extraction contains substantive content; and
- OpenIsle list and detail endpoints resolve the same literature paper ID.

Data points may be empty for theoretical, editorial or review papers and do not by themselves fail the audit.
Missing institution metadata is reported but does not fail an otherwise complete paper because many source APIs
do not supply affiliations reliably.

## Failure handling

### Retryable

- request timeout or connection reset;
- upstream rate limiting or temporary 5xx response;
- temporary PDF download failure;
- LLM timeout; and
- incomplete or truncated structured LLM output.

Retry attempts are bounded and visible. Exhausted items remain manually retryable.

### Terminal

- paywalled or unavailable full text;
- no open-PDF location after all configured resolvers;
- downloaded content is not a valid PDF;
- metadata lacks a usable identity; or
- the PDF demonstrably belongs to a different paper.

Terminal failures remain in the audit report but do not loop indefinitely or count toward the 30 complete papers.

### Incomplete content

- parsed text below the audit threshold;
- abstract-only content;
- missing knowledge card or methods record; or
- title/PDF mismatch.

Incomplete items are excluded from demo scoring until corrected and re-audited.

## Verification strategy

1. Python tests cover collection continuation, deduplication, state classification, retry and completeness audit.
2. Java tests cover rating create/update, snapshot status, formal thresholds and ranking order.
3. API checks compare literature-service and OpenIsle responses for the same paper IDs and artifacts.
4. Chrome regression covers login, list, detail, PDF, extracted knowledge, evaluation and rankings.
5. A restart test stops and starts the Compose stack and rechecks corpus and rating counts.

## Execution order

1. Add completeness/audit status and retry observability.
2. Add the repeatable batch collection and audit runner.
3. Collect and process candidates until 30 complete records are available.
4. Diagnose, fix and retry recoverable failures.
5. Add and run the idempotent demo-rating seeder.
6. Verify formal paper and author rankings.
7. Perform Chrome regression on at least 10 records and the complete rating path.
8. Restart Docker and verify persistence.
9. Generate and commit the final demonstration report.

## Safety and environment boundaries

- Demo rating generation is disabled unless an explicit development/demo environment flag is present.
- No production key, password or token is committed.
- Existing real users and ratings are not deleted or overwritten.
- Docker volumes are preserved; validation never uses `docker compose down -v`.
